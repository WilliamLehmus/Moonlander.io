🌕 Moonlander.io Operations Manual

Welcome, Pilot. Here is everything you and your crew need to survive the cavernous depths of the Moon.

🎯 1. Ultimate Mission Goal

Your objective is to venture 5,000 meters deep into the lunar crust to reach the Moon Core. Work together as a team to mine resources, construct outposts, run power/fuel lines, and maintain radar coverage as you descend through treacherous biomes.

🚀 2. Flight, Docking & EVA

  • Pilot Controls: Use W (Thrust), A / D (Rotate), and L (Spotlight).
  • Landing & Docking: Touch down gently on any powered Landing Pad. Docking immediately repairs your hull, recharges your battery, and dispenses fuel (starts with a 500-unit fuel reserve).
  • EVA Mode (Astronaut): Hold X for 2 seconds to exit your ship. In EVA, walk around on foot, build outposts, or haul cable spools.
  • Hitchhiking (Co-Op Rescue): An astronaut in EVA can press E to enter any teammate's lander as a passenger!

⛏️ 3. Mining & Ore Refining

  • Mining Laser: Hold SPACE near cave walls to mine ore deposits. Higher tier ores are found deeper down.
  • Ore Tiers: Iron & Copper (Shallow), Silver & Titanium (Deep), Gold & Platinum (Abyssal), Diamond & Helium-3 (Core). Bitite is a hydrocarbon ore found at all depths.
  • Refining: The Habitat has a built-in starter refinery (3 units/sec, self-powering). Delivered ores automatically refine into building materials (Basic, Industrial, Advanced, Quantum).
  • Fuel Synthesis: Bitite ore is refined into liquid lander fuel to keep your fleet flying!

🏗️ 4. Base Building & The Base Bus

  • Landing Pad Anchor: Every base begins with a Landing Pad. Placing a pad creates a 200m building radius.
  • The Base Bus: Buildings placed on the pad deck (within 200m and ±25m elevation) are automatically wired to all 3 networks without cables!
  • Underground Outposts: Buildings placed in deep caves or off the deck require physical cable connections to function.

🔌 5. The Three-Network Cable System

Bases run on 3 independent networks. The color defines the function:

🔴 Power Cable (Red)

Distributes electricity (kW flow & kJ buffer). Powered by Habitat (15 kW starter), Solar Arrays (10 kW, surface only), or Fuel Generators (50 kW, burns fuel).

🟢 Fuel Pipe (Green)

Transfers liquid fuel from refineries to Fuel Depots, Landing Pads, and Fuel Generators. A pad with no fuel line cannot refuel ships once its local tank is dry!

🔵 Data Cable (Blue)

Merges radar networks across distant outposts into a unified team Data Net.

💡 Load Shedding: If power is deficient, the base automatically shuts off low-priority buildings (Lights → Crafting → Refinery → Antenna → Landing Pad) to protect vital systems.

📡 6. Radar & Minimap Visibility

  • Explored Terrain: Permanent memory shown as desaturated static on the minimap.
  • Live Signals: Active radar blips (players, landers, hazards, dropped items) are ONLY visible inside the coverage bubble of a powered Comm Antenna (100m+) or Habitat (250m).
  • Data Sharing: Linking antennas with 🔵 Blue Data Cables merges their radar feeds. Teammates linked to the same Data Net see each other's live blips across the entire cavern!

🤝 7. Cooperative Systems & Rescue

  • Physics Tethers: Press G to attach a tow rope to another ship, wreckage, or stranded teammate. Tow out-of-fuel landers back to base!
  • Inventory Sharing: Open inventory (Q) near another ship or station to transfer ores, fuel, and spare parts.
